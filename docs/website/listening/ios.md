# iPhone and iPad audio settings

**Verified against linked Apple documentation: 2026-11-11.** Options depend on
the iOS/iPadOS version, AirPods model, and app. OCF cannot detect or disable
device processing for you.

## Baseline

- Use comfortable stereo headphones or earbuds when the selected edition is
  designed for separate left and right channels. Wired headphones are a simple
  option; compatible Bluetooth stereo headphones can also work.
- Keep left/right balance centered unless you use an accessibility adjustment.
- Start at a comfortable, clearly audible level. Keep normal playback speed
  (1.0x); turn off unwanted repeat, shuffle, crossfade, and optional EQ/effects.
- Do not raise volume to chase a perceived effect.

## AirPods spatial audio

1. Connect and wear compatible AirPods.
2. Open Control Center.
3. Touch and hold the volume control.
4. Tap **Spatial Audio**, then choose **Off** rather than **Fixed** or **Head
   Tracked** when available.

Apple notes that Spatial Audio controls depend on supported models and apps. See
[Control Spatial Audio and head tracking](https://support.apple.com/guide/airpods/control-spatial-audio-and-head-tracking-dev00eb7e0a3/web).

## Mono audio, balance, and headphone accommodations

Open **Settings → Accessibility → Audio & Visual** to review Mono Audio and the
left/right balance. Headphone Accommodations are optional accessibility
settings. Do not disable an accessibility setting you rely on; use the playback
format and text alternatives that work for you. See [Apple's iPhone audio
accessibility guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/iphb80ab7516/ios).

## Troubleshooting

If sound seems mono or distorted, verify the selected output and balance, then
inspect the current app's playback and AirPods settings. Some spatial settings
are per app. Do not increase volume beyond a comfortable level.
